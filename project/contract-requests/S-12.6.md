# Contract request — S-12.6 (quest view)

S-12.6 worked around this; nothing in `contracts/` was changed.

1. **Planned steps on a progress report** (`app-api.yaml` `ProgressEventData`, `channel.schema.json` `report`).
   The quest view shows the worker's steps as a checklist: ✓ done, ▶ now, · next (wireframe 2A). A `progress`
   event carries only `text`, so the app knows what was done and what it is doing now, but never what comes next.
   Today "· next" shows only before the first report ("Waiting for its turn"). Proposed: an optional
   `next: [string]` (the steps the worker still plans, in order) on `report(progress)` and on `ProgressEventData`.
   The app would list them under the current step with "·", and replace them with each new report.
