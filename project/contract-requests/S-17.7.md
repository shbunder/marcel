# Contract request — S-17.7: the brain should know its own animal

**What.** `contracts/runner-api.yaml`, `PrepareBrain.persona`: add an optional `animal` (the look's animal, app-api
`LookAnimal`), next to `personality`. 0.1.11 → 0.1.12.

**Why.** The brain's persona speaks as its animal ("a friendly pink giraffe … One giraffe joke is plenty"). `brain/render.py`
now takes `ANIMAL` (default `pink giraffe`, the owner's). Without the animal in `PrepareBrain`, the runner cannot pass it,
so a family member's brain speaks as a giraffe while the app draws its real look (live on the NUC: Emma's agent Pip, a
rabbit, said "a friendly pink giraffe").

**Who reads it.** The runner (`brains.py` passes `ANIMAL`; a one-line change once the field exists) and the hub
(`brain._cwd_for` sends `agent.look_animal`). No response change.
