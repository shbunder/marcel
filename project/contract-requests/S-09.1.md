# Contract request — S-09.1 (scheduler and schedules API)

S-09.1 serves `/api/schedules` and the brain's `schedule_*` tools; nothing in `contracts/` was changed. Three gaps
the lead may want to close:

1. **Error codes for schedules in the app API** (`app-api.yaml` `Error.code`). The hub answers
   `schedule_not_found` (404) and `schedule_builtin` (409) on `/api/schedules/{id}`, the same codes brain-api.yaml
   already lists. The app API's list of codes does not name them ("new codes may be added", so this is allowed,
   but the app cannot know them).
   Proposed: add "schedules: `schedule_not_found`, `schedule_builtin`" to the `Error.code` description, and give
   `DELETE /schedules/{id}`'s 409 the description "A built-in digest; it can be paused, not deleted".

2. **No live event for schedule changes** (`events.schema.json`). A schedule made by Marcel from the conversation,
   or one whose `next_run_at` / `last_run_at` moves after a fire, reaches the app only when it re-fetches
   `GET /schedules`.
   Proposed: `schedule.updated` (payload `Schedule`) and `schedule.deleted` (`{id}`), sent on create, change,
   delete and fire. The hub would send them from `schedule.Scheduler`.

3. **"Did not run" is pushed as category `note`** (`PushNotification.category`). The contract keeps `broke` for
   system faults and says `note` covers the hub's own notices (as the usage guard does). A missed or late scheduled
   job sits between the two. The hub posts a `system` message in the main conversation and pushes it as `note`
   (`normal`), linking to that message, with collapse key `schedules_missed` or `late:<schedule id>`.
   Proposed: confirm `note`, or add a `schedule` category if the app should show these differently.
