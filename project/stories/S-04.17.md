# S-04.17 — Hub: act on the runner's Claude update events

runner-api 0.1.12 `runner.claude_changed` and `runner.supervisor_restarted` (S-02.11); the runner sends them after
S-03.12. Today `hub/src/marcel_hub/claude_updates.py` reads `GET /health` every minute and guesses which sessions a
restart ended from the ones the runner no longer lists.

do:
1. Add both types to `RUNNER_EVENT_TYPES` and check their fields as the contract says (`claude_change`,
   `supervisor_restart`). Remove them from `PENDING_RUNNER_EVENT_TYPES` in `hub/tests/test_runner_client.py`.
2. `runner.supervisor_restarted`: mark the tasks of exactly the sessions in `session_ids` as ended by the restart, and
   word the owner's one notice from `cause` (e.g. `upgrade`: "Claude Code updated itself and restarted; 2 quests
   stopped."). No `cause`: the plain "Claude's supervisor restarted".
3. `runner.claude_changed`: "updated away" when `to` is not `expected`, "back on the tested version" when it is,
   replacing the `/health` comparison. Keep reading `/health` once at start-up, for a change made while the hub was
   down.
4. Remove the guess from the gone sessions. Docs: `hub/README.md` § `claude_updates.py`.

done when:
- [ ] Test (fake runner): a `runner.supervisor_restarted` naming two sessions marks exactly those two tasks and sends
  one notice that names the cause; a third live task is untouched.
- [ ] Test: `runner.claude_changed` away and back gives one notice each; deleting the away notice fails the test.
