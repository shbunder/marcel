# S-12.15 — App: a workflow's steps in the quest view

app-api 0.7.3 `TaskEventType` `workflow_start`, `workflow_stop`, `workflow_agent_start`, `workflow_agent_stop`
(S-02.11). Today `QuestEntry` reads them as plain helper lines ("Helper started: …").

do:
1. Give the four types their own `QuestEntry` cases.
2. In the quest view, a workflow is one folded row: its `description` (else `name`), "3 of 10 done", and a
   finished/failed mark from `workflow_stop`. Opened, it lists each agent by `label`, grouped by `phase` when set, each
   with its `summary` once it is done. Agents are matched to their stop by `agent_id`.
3. Untrusted text rules as for every other row: plain text only, long summaries capped.

done when:
- [ ] Test: ten agent starts and seven stops fold into one row reading "7 of 10 done"; a `workflow_stop` `failed`
  shows the failed mark.
- [ ] A snapshot of the opened workflow row in light and dark.
- [ ] `make ios-check` green.
