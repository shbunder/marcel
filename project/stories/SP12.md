# SP12 — A public address with Tailscale Funnel, no admin

For F22 (ADR-013 question 1). The Mac App Store Tailscale app does not support Funnel; the open-source `tailscaled`
in user-space mode should, without admin. Check it on a Mac (non-admin user) and on Linux (the NUC, as the owner, no
sudo), with a free Tailscale account. Two sittings: the Mac first, then the NUC over SSH from the Mac.

**Steps:**
1. Download `tailscale` and `tailscaled` (static release) into a scratch folder. Run
   `tailscaled --tun=userspace-networking --statedir=<dir> --socket=<dir>/tailscaled.sock` as the user, then
   `tailscale --socket=… up` (record the login link flow) and `tailscale --socket=… funnel --bg --set-path /api
   http://127.0.0.1:7420/api` in front of a throwaway FastAPI app with `/api/health`, `/api/ws` (WebSocket) and
   `/channel`.
2. From a phone on mobile data: `/api/health` answers; the WebSocket stays open 10 minutes; `/channel` and
   `/api/../channel` (`curl --path-as-is`) do not reach the app.
3. From a `claude --cloud` session: `curl -X POST https://<name>.ts.net/api/session/report` reaches the app.
4. Close the Mac's lid for 10 minutes, open it; switch Wi-Fi; does the address answer again, and after how long, with
   no command run? Does the address stay the same after a reboot?
5. Note: what the first `funnel` asks for (the tailnet policy link), whether an auth key (`--auth-key`) can replace
   the browser login, and the bandwidth or connection limits seen.

**Pass:** steps 1–4 work without admin on both systems → S-22.8 builds on it. **Fallback:** the owner-hosted encrypted
relay (ADR-013, rejected options) gets its own decision.
