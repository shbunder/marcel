# S-06.7 — Rollover by context size, on request, and its history

Contract 0.6 answered the S-06.2 and S-12.9 requests. S-06.2 rolls over nightly, on a crash and on
request only.

do:
1. **By context size.** Read `usage` on the brain's `turn_end` (transcript.schema.json, runner-api 0.1.7)
   and roll over (reason `context_full`) once `input + cache_creation + cache_read + output` passes 70 %
   of the brain model's context window, between turns as today.
2. **On request.** `POST /api/agents/{agent_id}/brain/rollover` → 202 and `BrainKeeper.roll_over`
   (reason `manual`); 409 `rollover_running` while one waits for its hello; 503 `runner_unreachable`.
3. **History.** A small `brain_rollover` table (id `rol_…`, agent_id, at, reason, kept), written where the
   `brain.rollover` event is published; `GET /api/agents/{agent_id}/brain/rollovers?limit=` newest first,
   `[]` before the first.
4. **No lost answer.** A `reply` refused with `replaced` goes to the new brain as a `rollover` system event
   with `data: {unsent_reply, in_reply_to?}` (channel-protocol.md § Brain rollover).
done when:
- [ ] Both routes off the pending list in `hub/tests/test_contract_routes.py`.
- [ ] Tests: a turn past 70 % rolls over once; a manual rollover twice in a row is 409; the list survives
  more than 2000 later events; a replaced reply reaches the new brain.
- [ ] `make -C hub check` and `make -C plugins/marcel/tools check` green.
