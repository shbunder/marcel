# S-08.2 — APNs backend

do: Token-based APNs over HTTP/2 (`.p8` key from `deploy/.env.local`). Map `PushNotification` as the contract
says: `aps.alert` from title and body, the `category` as the APNs category (an `approval` push
carries the Approve and Deny actions), `apns-collapse-id` from `collapse_key`, and the deep link
and ids beside `aps`. The backend is disabled when no key is configured: `GET /me` then reports
`push.apns: false`, and the app registers an ntfy topic instead.

- [ ] Test: JWT signing and the payload shape for each category, with a mocked APNs endpoint.
