# S-22.17 — The app says plainly when the computer is asleep or offline

On a laptop, sleep is normal (ADR-013 question 4). The app must not look broken.

do:
1. After the hub has not answered for 20 s (two failed connects), the chat shows one line under the header: "Your Mac
   is asleep or offline. Marcel will catch up when it wakes." with "last seen 14:02". The computer's kind comes from
   `/health` (`os`), so Linux says "computer". Sending still works: the message waits on the phone, marked "waiting",
   and goes when the hub answers.
2. When the hub answers again the line goes, waiting messages are sent in order, and the chat, quests and items catch
   up from the stored cursor (`EventStream` already resumes with `since`).
3. No retry storm: connect attempts back off up to 60 s while in the foreground, and stop in the background.
4. **Waking up** (SP12): a laptop's public address can stay dead after a wake until the watchdog (S-22.8) restarts the
   connection, a few minutes. The hub sends a quiet notice through the push relay when its watchdog finds the address
   dead (`status: reconnecting`; the relay is outbound, so it works while the address does not). While it holds, the
   line reads "Your Mac is waking up; Marcel is reconnecting." instead of "asleep or offline"; it clears as in 2.
done when:
- [ ] Tests with a fake connection: the line appears after two failures and not after one; waiting messages are sent
      once, in order, after reconnect; the cursor resume asks for `since` the last event.
- [ ] On the iPhone with the Mac's lid closed for 5 minutes: the line shows; opening the lid shows "waking up" and
      clears it with no action within 4 minutes (watchdog included; the time is recorded in the commit message).
- [ ] A test: a `reconnecting` notice while unreachable shows the waking-up line; reconnecting clears it.
