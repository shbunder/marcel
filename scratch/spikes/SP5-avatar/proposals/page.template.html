Marcel, Ten Ways

Ten directions for Marcel's avatar, from pixel art to high-quality 3D, each shown inside the kind of app it suits. The 2D ones are drawn live in code; the 3D ones are modelled and rendered in Blender (Cycles, real fur) and played as animation. Use the bar below to switch what Marcel is doing in all of them at once.
