At icon sizes

The test every good mascot passes: still a pink giraffe at 24 pixels. Shown as profile pictures.

Against the mascots people love

The yardstick: Duolingo's Duo ("a chicken nugget with a face"), OpenAI's Dots, and Clawd from Claude Code. What they share is a silhouette you know at icon size, two or three colours, a face that carries the emotion, and one signature detail.

How we got here

  • BoxesA 3D copy of the logo from rounded boxes. Faithful but lifeless.
  • Plush 3DSoft shapes with fur layers. The neck read as a balloon.
  • Pixel artA detailed 48 × 54 sprite. Cute up close, busy and small elsewhere.
  • Clawd styleA giraffe in Clawd's tall pixels. Too few pixels: it stopped being a giraffe.
  • Dot matrixThis design seen through round LED dots. The face fell apart at that resolution, so pixels stay a nod (rounded-square spots, four-point sparkles), not the medium.
  • FriendlyVector shapes: sharp at any size, smooth motion. Liked, but too childish for a family's assistant.
  • ProfessionalThe same giraffe, grown up: longer neck, calm eyes, no blush, tidy spots, a nod and a small wave instead of hops, chat-style bubbles instead of floating code.
